Turn on sync first

Browser Notifications needs Sync Across Devices turned on first. Both keep something for you on the Averages server, so they go together.

What turning this on means

To tell you about changes while Averages is closed, Averages checks your classes about every 20 minutes. To do that it stores your school sign-in key on its server, encrypted with a separate key that's only used for notifications. It keeps it only while notifications are on in one of your browsers.

Notifications only say what kind of thing changed, like "A new grade was posted." The details stay in Averages. Turn this off or sign out and the stored key is deleted right away.

Switch to Incognito?

Incognito mode stores nothing about you on our servers. It turns off Sync Across Devices and deletes what it stored, disconnects Canva, Google Drive and OneDrive, and turns off browser notifications. Files you saved to those apps stay in your accounts.

Turn off sync?

Your customizations (theme, background, accent color, and other settings) won't sync to your other devices anymore, and what's already saved to Averages.io's servers under your account gets deleted. Anything already on this device stays as-is.
