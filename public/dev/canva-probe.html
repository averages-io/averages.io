Canva permission probe

Developer only. Tries guessed Canva addresses for sharing a design with your own Canva connection. It only changes a new, empty test design and test folder it makes itself.

1. Connect Canva with the test permissions

Asks Canva for design:permission:read, design:permission:write and folder:permission:write on top of the usual ones. Canva comes back here.

StatusRequestCanva said
