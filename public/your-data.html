Your controls

Clear this device

Clear Cache in Settings removes the grades held for your current visit. To remove your saved profile and settings as well, clear site data for Averages.io in your browser.

Delete what we store

Turn off Sync Across Devices and notifications, and disconnect Canva, Google Drive and OneDrive, in Settings, or switch to Incognito mode, which does all of that. Everything they stored on our servers is deleted straight away.

Do school your way.

Averages.io is a student-built companion web app for your school's learning management system (LMS) that brings where you work to how you work. With Canva assignment editing built in, you can edit and submit without ever leaving the app. Averages.io also adds the features students ask for most, like projected grades, instant page loads and a customizable UI.
