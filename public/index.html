averages-io/averages.io Public

Do school your way.

Averages.io is a student-built companion web app for your school's learning management system (LMS) that brings where you work to how you work. With Canva assignment editing built in, you can edit and submit without ever leaving the app. Averages.io also adds the features students ask for most, like projected grades, instant page loads and a customizable UI.
