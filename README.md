# Averages.io

**Do school your way.**

Averages.io is a student-built companion web app for your school's learning
management system (LMS). It shows the courses, grades and assignments your school
already gives you access to, in a layout that is faster, friendlier and yours to
customize.

> **Averages.io is in beta.** The beta works with one learning platform, with more
> on the way. Star or watch this repo to follow along, and see
> [averages.io](https://averages.io) for what's new.

## What you get

- **A friendlier interface.** Your grades, assignments and today's calendar on one
  home screen, instead of spread across separate pages.
- **Projected grades.** Every course shows where it is heading, not just where it
  is, worked out from the trend in your recent graded work.
- **No waiting between pages.** Your data is held for as long as the app is open,
  so moving between Grades, Assignments and your calendar is instant.
- **Make it yours.** Pick your wallpaper and accent color, rename your courses and
  put them in the order you want.
- **Turn in work from the app.** Upload files, add a note, and turn them in
  without leaving the assignment.
- **Canva, Google Drive and OneDrive.** Open assignment files in Canva or Google
  Drive, edit them and turn them in, or add files straight from Google Drive and
  OneDrive.
- **And much more.** Gradebook breakdowns, a grade calculator, calendar, course
  materials, messages and dark mode.
- **Google Classroom is coming later.**

## Averages Plus

Everything above is free. Averages Plus is optional, $2.99 a month, and adds:

- browser notifications for new grades, assignments, due dates, messages and
  announcements;
- calendar sync, with your due dates in Google Calendar or Apple Calendar;
- new features about two weeks before everyone else;
- the iPhone app, when it comes out.

You can cancel anytime in Settings › Plus.

## Your privacy

- **Your grades are never stored on our servers.** They pass through on the way to
  your device and stop there.
- **Nothing is sold.** Not your grades, not your name, not anything else.
- **You choose how much is kept.** In Incognito mode nothing about you is stored on
  our servers. In Classic mode your settings follow you to every device you sign in
  on.
- **Signing out cleans up.** Your data is cleared from that device, and Canva,
  Google Drive and OneDrive are disconnected.
- **Photos are checked on your device.** Profile photos and backgrounds are
  screened for inappropriate content without ever being uploaded for the check.

The details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://averages.io/privacy-policy), and
everything kept about you is listed on the [Your data](https://averages.io/your-data)
page.

## API

The app never talks to your school's platform directly. It goes through its own
API, which is open source too:
**[averages-io/averages.api](https://github.com/averages-io/averages.api)**.

That's where sign-in, the data each page shows and Sync Across Devices live, so
you can check exactly what is sent to your school and what is kept. It's under
the same GPL v3 license as this repo.
